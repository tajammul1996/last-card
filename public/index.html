        House rules

        How to play

        • Everyone starts with 7 cards. Play one that matches the top card’s colour, number or symbol. Wilds go on anything and you pick the next colour.
        • Can’t play? Tap the draw pile. If the new card fits you can play it, or pass.
        • ⊘ Skip jumps the next player. ⇄ Reverse flips direction (with 2 players it acts like Skip). +2 and Wild +4 make the next player draw.
        • With stacking on, answer a +2 or +4 with another; whoever can’t, draws the total.
        • Down to two cards? Tap Last card! before you play. Forget, and anyone can tap Catch: you draw 2.
        • First to empty their hand wins the round. Wins count on the leaderboard when you’re signed in.
        • On a computer: press 1–9 to play a card, D to draw, L for Last card, C to chat.
