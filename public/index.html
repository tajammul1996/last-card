How a turn works

  • Play a card that matches the top card's color, number or symbol. Wilds go on anything.
  • Can't play? Tap the draw pile. If the drawn card fits you may play it, or pass.
  • ⊘ Skip jumps the next player, ⇄ Reverse flips direction, +2 and Wild +4 make the next player draw.
  • Down to two cards? Tap Last card! before you play. Forget, and anyone can catch you for +2.
